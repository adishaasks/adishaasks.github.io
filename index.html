About the channel

Adisha is an animated cartoon character who asks big questions. Two robot friends answer her. Brahma tells what the old stories say, and AI explains what science has found. Each topic comes as a pair of short videos in Kannada, made for curious learners of every age.

Adisha Channel Tools

Adisha Channel Tools is the private software the channel's owner uses to publish the channel's own videos. It is not a public app, and nobody else can sign in to it.

With the owner's permission, it connects to these services:

  • YouTube (YouTube API Services): uploads the channel's own videos and thumbnails, and reads the channel's own view statistics.
  • Instagram and Facebook (Meta Graph API): publishes the channel's own Reels and posts, and reads their statistics.

It works only with accounts that belong to this channel. It does not collect any information about viewers. Read the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service for details.
